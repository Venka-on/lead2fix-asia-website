Installing lead lined partition walls

We can install the panels we supply. These are the general steps. The exact method follows your project specification and your radiation consultant's requirements.

Our installation steps

  1. Check drawings and site

    We review the drawings, the wall layout and the specified lead thickness before work starts.

  2. Build the frame

    A metal stud frame is set out for the partition.

  3. Fix the lead lined boards

    The boards are screwed to the frame, row by row.

  4. Shield joints and corners

    Joints, corners and frame members are covered with lead as the specification requires, so the shielding has no gaps.

  5. Seal openings

    Pipes, cables, sockets and other openings are fitted with lead sealing.

  6. Finish and hand over

    The wall is finished like normal plasterboard. Your radiation consultant carries out any checks needed before the room is used.

What we need from you

  • Drawings and the wall layout
  • The lead thickness specified by your radiation physicist or consultant
  • The site address and access arrangements
  • Your target schedule
