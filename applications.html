Rooms that use lead lined walls

Lead lined partitions are used where equipment or materials give off radiation.

X-ray and dental rooms

Clinics that use X-ray equipment may need shielded walls. A radiation protection expert sets the shielding level.

CT, PET/CT and nuclear medicine rooms

Rooms with CT or PET/CT scanners, and rooms that handle radioactive materials, may need thicker lead. The level is set by a radiation protection expert.

Operating theatres

Theatres with X-ray or other imaging equipment may need lead lined walls.

Laboratories, security scanning rooms and industrial radiation areas can also need lead lined walls. Each room needs its own specification.
