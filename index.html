Why use lead lined plasterboard

  • The lead is laminated to the plasterboard, so board and lead go onto the frame together.
  • Panels fix to a metal stud frame using normal drywall methods.
  • Panels are ordered to suit your wall layout, which helps limit offcuts. We confirm sizes when we quote.
  • One company for the panels and the installation.

The lead thickness is set by your radiation physicist or consultant, not by us.
