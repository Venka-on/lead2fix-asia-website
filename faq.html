Questions about lead lined partition walls

Short answers to common questions. For anything specific to your room, ask your radiation consultant.

Who decides how much lead is needed?

Your radiation physicist or consultant sets it. We supply panels to that specification.

Do you only supply, or install too?

Both. We supply the panels, and we can also install them. Tell us which you need.

What do the panels fix to?

Metal stud frames, using normal drywall fixing methods.

Can panels be made to a specific size?

We confirm panel sizes when we quote. Send us your wall layout.

Is the finished wall approved for use?

Approval depends on your room, the specification and the relevant authority. Your radiation consultant confirms this. We do not give approvals ourselves.

What are the price and lead time?

They depend on panel sizes, lead thickness and quantity. We give a quote after seeing your requirements.

What do I send to get a quote?

Your wall layout or panel list, the specified lead thickness, the quantity and your site address.

Tell us what you need

Send your wall layout, the lead thickness your radiation physicist has specified and your site address, and we will prepare a quote.
